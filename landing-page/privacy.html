Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 31, 2026. Your health data is yours alone. We design our software to store your logs locally and securely.

Data Integrity Principles

  • 🔒 Local-First Storage: Your coffee logging history and configuration parameters are stored locally on your device via secure `UserDefaults` or standard keychain namespaces.
  • ☁️ Private CloudKit Sync: For Ultra subscribers, iCloud synchronization is orchestrated directly between your Macs using your personal private Apple CloudKit containers. We do not operate external database servers, and we never see your sync logs.
  • ❤️ Apple Health: If you authorize the Apple Health integration, CaffeineBar writes caffeine content (dietary caffeine) directly to Apple Health on your request. CaffeineBar has **write-only** access; we never read your other Health metrics.
  • ❌ No Analytics or Tracking: There are no analytics trackers, advertising identifiers, or performance diagnostics reporting tools compiled into CaffeineBar. We don't track your screen views, habits, or locations.

Purchase Information

Payments for direct downloads are processed securely by Polar.sh. When you buy Pro or Ultra, we are notified of the license state, but we do not receive or hold credit card details.
