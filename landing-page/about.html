About CaffeineBar

CaffeineBar is a modern, visual coffee cup counter for macOS. We believe in visual clarity, simple workflows, and helping you build a healthier relationship with your daily brews.

Differentiating CaffeineBar

There are several apps in the macOS ecosystem with similar names (e.g., "Caffeine", "Caffeinated", "Amphetamine") whose primary function is to prevent your Mac from sleeping or going into screen-saver mode.

CaffeineBar is NOT a sleep-prevention utility.

We do not alter your Mac's screen-saver, display sleep cycles, or system suspension behavior. CaffeineBar is purely a metabolic calculator and log for your actual caffeine intake. It runs silently in your menu bar to count your daily cups of coffee, model your active caffeine clearance curves, and warn you if your late-afternoon consumption is going to disrupt your nighttime sleep.

Why track coffee?

Caffeine has a half-life of 5 hours for the average adult. This means 10 hours after a double espresso, a quarter of the caffeine is still active in your brain, blocking adenosine receptors and preventing high-quality deep sleep. By modeling this curve interactively, we empower you to enjoy coffee when it helps, and skip it when it hurts.
